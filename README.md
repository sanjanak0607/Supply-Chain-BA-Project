# Supply Chain Analysis Project

### SQL | Excel | Business Analysis | Supply Chain Insights

---

## Project Overview

This is an end-to-end Supply Chain Analytics project built using a supply chain dataset containing product, supplier, inventory, shipping, and quality-related information.

The objective was to analyze operational performance across the supply chain, identify business risks, uncover actionable insights, and provide data-driven recommendations using SQL and Excel.

The project focuses on four critical business areas:

* Supplier Quality Performance
* Inventory & Stockout Risk
* Shipping & Logistics Performance
* Revenue & Profitability Analysis

---

## Dataset

* **Source:** Kaggle Supply Chain Dataset
* **Size:** 100 Products | 5 Suppliers
* **Industry:** Supply Chain & Logistics
* **Key Attributes:** Revenue, Defect Rate, Stock Levels, Shipping Costs, Lead Times, Inspection Results, Transportation Modes
